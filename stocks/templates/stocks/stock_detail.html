📖 Stock Market Glossary — Beginner's Guide
📈 Stock / Share
A small piece of ownership in a company. If Apple has 1 billion shares and you own 1, you own 1 billionth of Apple.
💰 Stock Price
The current cost to buy one share. Determined by what buyers are willing to pay and sellers are willing to accept.
📊 % Change
How much the price moved today vs. yesterday's closing price. +2% means it rose 2%. -3% means it fell 3%.
📉 Volume
Total number of shares traded in a day. High volume means lots of people are buying/selling — the stock is "active".
🌅 Opening Price
The first price when trading begins each morning at 9:30 AM Eastern Time (New York).
🌙 Previous Close
The last price when trading ended the previous day at 4:00 PM Eastern Time.
📐 Moving Average (SMA)
The average price over the last N days. Smooths out daily noise to reveal the real trend. Price above SMA 20 = uptrend.
⚡ RSI (Strength Index)
A score from 0–100. Above 70 = "overbought" (might drop). Below 30 = "oversold" (might rise). 40–60 = normal range.
🌊 Volatility
How "jumpy" a stock is. High volatility = large price swings (risky). Low volatility = stable, steady price movements.
🐂 Bullish
Expecting the price to go UP. A "bull market" is when prices are generally rising.
🐻 Bearish
Expecting the price to go DOWN. A "bear market" is when prices are generally falling (down 20%+ from recent high).
📡 Watchlist
Your personal list of stocks you want to monitor. You don't own them — you just track their price movements.
